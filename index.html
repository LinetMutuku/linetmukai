Process

How an engagement actually goes.

No mystery, no slide deck of buzzwords. Four phases, paid discovery up front, weekly demos throughout.

01 · Discovery

Listen & map

A paid one-week discovery: I read the codebase, interview the team, audit the product, and produce a written tech plan with risks and budget.

02 · Shape

Prototype & align

Working prototypes — not Figma flat-files — for the critical flows. Designed alongside your team, in real code, on real devices.

03 · Build

Ship in slices

Weekly increments behind a feature flag. Storybook for the design system, e2e tests for the workflows, Lighthouse budgets in CI.

04 · Handover

Document & teach

Written architecture notes, recorded walkthroughs, and a 30-day post-launch retainer included. Your team owns the codebase, fully.
